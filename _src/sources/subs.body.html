Whether this fits your account

The model trades whole shares and sizes every position as a percentage of its own equity. Below a certain account size it cannot take some of its own trades, so a smaller account follows the model less closely. This is a measured property of the model, not a rule imposed on you.

Account sizeWhat happens
Under $35,000Can follow, but some stock prices are unaffordable at the model's own weights, so some skipped trades will result
$50,000Comfortable. Most of what the model does is reproducible
$250,000 and upEffortless. The model's trades are reproducible without friction

Questions

What is QuickStart?

A new Moyogi subscriber who wishes to follow the model builds into the portfolio gradually, each new position alerted as the model opens it, so it can be months before their account resembles the model's if that is what they wish to do. QuickStart hands a new Yamadori subscriber the model's full portfolio and its weights at signup, so they can see the whole book on day one instead.

How often does a first-cycle name actually happen?

Promotions are the output of a quarterly review, not a daily one. Some quarters bring several and some bring none.

Is this advice?

No. Kodama is a research publication and is not financial advice. We are only sharing what the model has traded, prepared without any knowledge of your financial situation, your holdings, your account, your goals and objectives or your tax position. We are not your adviser, and every alert reports what the model did rather than telling you what to do. What you do with this research is your decision and your responsibility.

Are the returns real money?

No. The published record is a hypothetical model portfolio that runs the rules mechanically. It is not an account, not a fund, and nobody's money produced those historical returns. The Kodama Trade Desk monitors it themselves on a going-forward basis only.

Your own results may differ, because the model pays no commission, trades at open prices, suffers no slippage, pays no tax, and never hesitates on a bad morning.

What currency are the prices in?

US dollars, for everyone, including subscribers in Canada. The research covers US equities and the record is kept in US dollars, so the pricing follows. Sales tax or an equivalent may be added at checkout depending on where you live.
